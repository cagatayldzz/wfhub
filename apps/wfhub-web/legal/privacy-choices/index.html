App preferences

You can change supported preferences in the app’s Settings. Desktop settings are stored locally in ~/.config/wfhub/settings.json; iOS preferences are stored by the operating system.

Third-party services

The apps need network access to load live information and artwork. Opening external links takes you to third-party services, where their policies and controls apply.
