Privacy policy

How WFHub handles information when you use the project.

Draft for review: the site operator must confirm hosting, retention, contact details, and applicable legal requirements before publishing this policy.

Information on your device

The desktop application saves preferences such as your Tenno name, platform, and appearance on your device. The iOS app stores preferences using the platform’s local settings. These settings are separate between devices and applications.

Network requests

The apps request world-state data, Codex data, market information, and images from external providers. Those providers and the network may receive technical information such as your IP address when a request is made. Their own policies apply to their services.

External services include Warframe Status, Warframe Market, and WFHub data and image endpoints.

This website

This site is built as static pages. Hosting providers may process technical request information as part of delivering the site. Hosting configuration and retention should be confirmed before publication.
